Preferred Role(s) Moving Forward

Rating Scale

  1. 1 – Unfamiliar / Needs Instruction: Have not practiced or do not feel safe attempting.
  2. 2 – Developing: Can perform with direct instructor coaching and extra time.
  3. 3 – Competent: Can perform accurately under calm, controlled training conditions.
  4. 4 – Proficient: Can perform reliably under stress, cold water, or zero visibility.
  5. 5 – Mastery: Ready for live operations; capable of assisting or mentoring others.

Self-ratings are not operational authorization. Team leadership determines deployment readiness under applicable SOPs.
